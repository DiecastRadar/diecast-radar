INSTALL TO YOUR PHONE

Once you publish your live link (via GitHub Pages), open it on your mobile browser and follow these steps to install the app icon directly onto your phone:

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the Three Dots Menu in the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  3. Confirm to place the Diecast Radar icon on your launcher!
